SMALL APP. YOUR MAC.

Make yourself
at home.

Your saved sites and preferences stay in a local file on your Mac. No account, sync service, or analytics. An optional Chrome extension adds a toolbar save button.

Choose Open in Chrome or TopChrome on the shelf. Chrome can reuse a dedicated window or create a new one. TopChrome opens each site as a new tab in its drawer. The gear button opens the rest of your preferences.

Works with TopChrome. Save Current Chrome Tab also works without an extension.

Build locally with Swift 6 and the Xcode command-line tools. The development bundle is ad-hoc signed. Signed and notarized downloads will be published separately.
